# FAQ

Why every Gmail or Outlook account can stop working at once, and what to do.

- **Gmail users who sign in with Google have to sign in again every
  week.** Your Google app is still in *Testing*. Publish it, then have
  each user sign in once more: see
  [Stop the weekly re-sign-in: publish your app](providers/google.md#stop-the-weekly-re-sign-in-publish-your-app).
- **Every Outlook account suddenly fails.** The Entra client secret has
  probably expired (they last at most 24 months); create a new one as
  described in [Create a client secret](providers/microsoft.md#2-create-a-client-secret).
