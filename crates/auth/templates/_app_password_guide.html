{% if mail.icloud %}

iCloud Mail needs an app-specific password, not your Apple Account password. This server never sees your real password.

  1. Sign in at account.apple.com

    Two-factor authentication must be on. Most Apple Accounts already have it.

    Open account.apple.com (opens in a new tab)

  2. Open Sign-In and Security, then App-Specific Passwords

    You may be asked for your Apple Account password again.

  3. Generate one named “postkeep”

    It looks like abcd-efgh-ijkl-mnop and is shown only once.

  4. Paste it into the form

    Dashes and spaces are fine. Back to the form

Good to know
  • The password is checked with iCloud before anything is saved, then stored encrypted. It is used only for IMAP and SMTP.
  • To disconnect, delete it under App-Specific Passwords at account.apple.com. Access stops immediately.
  • Changing your Apple Account password revokes every app-specific password. Create a new one and connect again.

Apple's instructions: Sign in to apps with your Apple Account using app-specific passwords (opens in a new tab)

Your provider needs an app password, created in its security settings. Your regular password won't work.

  1. Open your provider's security settings

    Two-step verification usually has to be on first.

    {%- for (name, url) in mail.other_guides %}{{ name }} app passwords (opens in a new tab) {% endfor %}

  2. Create an app password named “postkeep”

    Copy it; most providers show it only once.

  3. Paste it into the form

    It's checked with your provider before anything is saved. Back to the form

Only TLS connections are used. The servers are fixed by whoever runs this postkeep, never typed in here.
