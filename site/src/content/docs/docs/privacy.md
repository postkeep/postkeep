**Effective date: November 28, 2026**

postkeep.dev is the site of the open-source postkeep server. It is run by postkeep, the trade name of a sole proprietorship based in the Philippines, which also runs postkeep Cloud. You can reach us at `support@postkeep.ai`.

This note covers postkeep.dev only. The postkeep server you run yourself sends us nothing: no telemetry. postkeep Cloud, the hosted service, has its own [Privacy Policy](https://postkeep.ai/privacy).

## What this site keeps

- No accounts, forms or advertising.
- **Visits.** Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits and measures how fast pages load: for each page view, the page and the site that linked to it, how fast the page loaded, your browser, operating system and kind of device, and your country. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Cloudflare keeps every page view's measurements for 7 days and a sample of them after that, which we can see for 6 months.
- **Your theme.** If you pick a theme, your browser's local storage keeps your choice, under `postkeep-theme`, so the next page uses it. It never leaves your browser. Clear the site's data in your browser to remove it.
- **Cloudflare** serves the site. It handles your IP address and request details to deliver and protect the pages, under [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/), and may ask your browser to report network errors to it.

Where the GDPR applies, our basis is our legitimate interest in running a working, safe site and seeing how it is used. Your rights, and how to use them, are in [section 12 of postkeep Cloud's Privacy Policy](https://postkeep.ai/privacy#your-rights).

Questions: email `support@postkeep.ai`.
