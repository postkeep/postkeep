iCloud Mail connects with an **app-specific password**: it doesn't accept
your Apple Account password from other apps.

:::caution
Your Apple Account must use two-factor authentication.
:::

<Steps>

1. Sign in at [account.apple.com](https://account.apple.com).

2. Under **Sign-In and Security**, choose **App-Specific Passwords**.

3. Choose **Generate an app-specific password** and follow the steps
   ([Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102654)).

4. Copy the password (it looks like `abcd-efgh-ijkl-mnop`) and paste it on
   postkeep's sign-in page, with or without the dashes.

</Steps>

To remove postkeep's access, see [Disconnect](/docs/clients/#disconnect).
Changing your Apple Account password revokes every app-specific password:
sign in again on `/account` with a new one. Signing in there with a new
password replaces the stored one and signs out other browsers on the
account page.

iCloud doesn't save mail sent over SMTP to Sent Messages, so
`send_message` and `reply_to_message` save a copy there unless
`save_to_sent` is `false`.
